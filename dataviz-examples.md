## Part 3: 2024 Government Debt-to-GDP Ratios
This visualization focuses on the 20 countries with the highest government debt-to-GDP ratios in 2024. Greece and Italy are highlighted to draw attention to the two highest ratios, while the remaining countries are shown in a muted color for context. The 100% reference line provides an additional benchmark for comparing debt levels across countries.
